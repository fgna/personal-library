# Genre taxonomy

Personal Library stores `genre` as a small controlled set so genre filters stay stable across imports, scans and edits.

A book may have up to two top-level genre categories:

- Belletristik
- Krimi & Thriller
- Fantasy & Science-Fiction
- Biografie
- Geschichte
- Politik & Gesellschaft
- Wirtschaft
- Psychologie
- Philosophie
- Wissenschaft
- Gesundheit
- Ratgeber
- Reise
- Kinder & Jugend
- Religion
- Kunst & Kultur

Detailed subject terms belong in `keywords`, not in `genre`. Android scan enrichment may only choose genre values from this list and only when they are supported by grounded source metadata.
